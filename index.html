How I design

Feeling first. Then cut.

Decide the feeling

Before a mechanic exists, I decide what the player should feel. Everything that doesn’t help that feeling goes, even if it works.

See it in the Raven chain →

Let moments breathe

Not every moment at the same intensity. Pauses, rhythm and sound decide how hard a win lands, and how much a near-miss stings.

See it in the big-win counter →

Rebuild when it’s wrong

I threw away the whole first reel structure because it worked but didn’t feel right. It was the best decision of the project.

See it in the dev log →
About

Hi, I’m Jon.

I’m a game designer from Spain, based in Tokyo. My strongest interests are art, mechanics and sensations: how a world looks, what happens inside it and how each moment lands.

  • From idea to playable. Ossuary Reels is mine from the first sketch: the world, the board, every rule and every bonus.
  • Mechanics with a purpose. I connect rules, risk and reward to the experience I want to create, then iterate on what feels unclear or flat.
  • Art direction & game feel. I shape the visual language, animation and sound. I use AI coding tools to build my specifications, then review and iterate on the result.

Business · Studios, operators & publishers

My games are for sale.
So is my head.

Ossuary Reels is an original concept: the world, the ribcage board, the bonus systems and their math, designed by me. There are three ways to work with it, or with me.

Licensing Nº 01

License the game

For operators, aggregators and slot studios.

Put Ossuary Reels in your lobby. You or your production partner handle certification and integration; I bring the design, the math model and the art direction, and stay on to tune the game.

  • Exclusive or non-exclusive
  • Revenue share or fixed fee
  • Design support through certification
Ask about licensing
Acquisition Nº 02

Buy the concept

For studios looking for their next title.

Take the idea and make it yours: the world, the board, the bonus features and the design documentation, ready for your team to produce in your own engine and art style.

  • Full rights or adaptation rights
  • Design documents and math model
  • Handover sessions with your team
