About

Hi, I’m Jon.

I’m from Spain, and for the past year I’ve lived in Tokyo, where I study Japanese. For ten years I made physical things: welding prototypes at Mercedes-Benz, machining and maintaining industrial equipment, and today desserts, as a pastry chef at EDITION Tokyo. All of it taught me the same thing: details decide how something feels.

I haven’t worked at a game studio yet. Ossuary Reels is how I show what I can do: I had the idea, designed every system, directed the art and the sound, and built it with AI tools, testing and rebuilding until it felt right.

I’m creative and a bit obsessive. When something doesn’t feel right I can’t leave it alone, and I’m not afraid to throw work away to get it there.

How I design

Feeling first. Then cut.

Decide the feeling

Before a mechanic exists, I decide what the player should feel. Everything that doesn’t help that feeling goes, even if it works.

Let moments breathe

Not every moment at the same intensity. Pauses, rhythm and sound decide how hard a win lands, and how much a near-miss stings.

Rebuild when it’s wrong

I threw away the whole first reel structure because it worked but didn’t feel right. It was the best decision of the project.
