About

Hi, I’m Jon.

I’m a game designer from Spain, based in Tokyo. I design slot features end to end: what triggers them, how they pay, how often they land, and what the player feels while waiting for them.

  • From idea to playable. Ossuary Reels is mine from the first sketch: the world, the board, every rule and every bonus.
  • Systems with numbers behind them. Every feature has hit-rate and payout targets, and I retune them when playtesting says they feel wrong.
  • Direction. I direct the art, the animation and the sound, and I build with AI coding tools: I define it, test it and send it back until it’s right.

Design breakdowns

The Raven chain

How do you make a free spin feel earned? Every raven the stone kills grants one more spin, and the chain goes on while ravens keep falling.

Probability · Pacing
02

Blades & the Chest

Cut symbols leave holes that break lines, and fall into a chest. When it’s full enough, it opens: collect, or gamble it on a wheel.

Systems · Risk & reward
03

The Execution Bonus

A respin chain to unlock it, three candles to survive it, and a guillotine multiplier that grows with every hand you cut.

Bonus design · Tension
04

A big win, cut to the music

The counter is timed to its track: small wins land fast and quiet, big ones ride the music and stop exactly on a cymbal hit.

How I design

Feeling first. Then cut.

Decide the feeling

Before a mechanic exists, I decide what the player should feel. Everything that doesn’t help that feeling goes, even if it works.

See it in the Raven chain →

Let moments breathe

Not every moment at the same intensity. Pauses, rhythm and sound decide how hard a win lands, and how much a near-miss stings.

See it in the big-win counter →

Rebuild when it’s wrong

I threw away the whole first reel structure because it worked but didn’t feel right. It was the best decision of the project.
