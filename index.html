About · What I bring to a studio

Hi, I’m Jon.

I’m a game designer from Spain, based in Tokyo. My strongest interests are art, mechanics and sensations: how a world looks, what happens inside it and how each moment lands.

Ossuary Reels is my hands-on design case study, from the reel layout and bonus systems to art direction and playtesting. These decisions demonstrate how I work as a designer.

  • From idea to playable. I designed the world, the reel layout and the bonus rules for Ossuary Reels.
  • Mechanics with a purpose. I connect rules, risk and reward to the experience I want to create, then iterate on what feels unclear or flat.
  • Art direction & game feel. I set the art direction and specify animation and audio timing. I use AI coding tools to implement the design, then review and playtest it.
